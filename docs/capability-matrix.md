## Status taxonomy

| Badge | Name | Definition |
|-------|------|------------|
| ✅ | **Implemented** | Computed by code, covered by tests, physics validated against analytical results |
| 🔶 | **Simplified** | Runs end-to-end but with documented approximations or reduced dimensionality; the assumption is stated inline wherever the capability is described |
| 🧪 | **Theoretical** | Parameterized and runnable, but models speculative physics; outputs are research projections, not validated engineering |
| 🗺️ | **Planned** | Not in code; roadmap only. Never described in prose as if it runs |

A split grade is written in one form everywhere: the headline badge first, then the qualified parts in parentheses with their own badge first — `✅ Implemented (🔶 coherence bound)` means the named capability is exact and tested while the part in parentheses rests on the stated approximation, and `🔶 Simplified (✅ lines, edges, Duane–Hunt, Be window)` names the exact parts of a simplified model. The first badge is always the headline grade: it is the colour of the row, of the tile in the map below, of the page's Status callout and of every chip that copies the row. Model-coverage tables on source/process pages additionally tag each struct field as `live` (enters a computation), `stored (inert)` (carried and exposed, awaiting a consumer), or `planned` (not yet a field).

The counts below are generated from the tables on this page (`docs/figures/reference/make_legend.py`); the same legend is printed on the home page and in the README.

## Audit log

The 2026-09-30 audit, its 2026-10-01 follow-up and the 2026-10-10 sampler fix corrected the defects below; "Before" describes what the code did, not what the documentation claimed. Corrected numbers are recorded here and never silently deleted: the withdrawn SRAF dipole DOF gains, the MNSL regrade and the DSA defect-index wording stay traceable through this table. The layout sweep of 2026-10-10 is logged last.
